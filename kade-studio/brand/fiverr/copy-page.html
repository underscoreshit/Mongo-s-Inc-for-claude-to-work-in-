Step 3. After publishing what the top sellers do

  1. Turn on Fiverr notifications on your phone. Reply within the hour; the response time is shown on your profile.
  2. Post the three gigs the same day. The cheap fix gig brings the first reviews.
  3. Deliver before the deadline, never on it. If a buyer goes quiet, click "Request extension" early.
  4. Never move the conversation or the payment outside Fiverr before the order is complete.
  5. After delivery, ask once for a review.
