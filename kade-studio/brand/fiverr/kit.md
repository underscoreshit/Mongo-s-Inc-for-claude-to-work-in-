# Fiverr kit for Kade Studio

Fiverr is international and English-first. Buyers search in English, so the profile and the gig are in English. Dutch and French are listed as languages and used as a selling point (bilingual sites).

## 1. Create the account (you, about 15 minutes)
1. fiverr.com → **Join** → with email (use the Kade address, not a personal one).
2. Username: **kadestudio** (or `kade_studio` if taken). It cannot be changed later.
3. Confirm email and phone number.
4. **Become a seller** → fill in the profile with the texts below.
5. Identity check: photo of your ID card + selfie. Required before your first payout.
6. Payout: **Payoneer** or **bank transfer** (IBAN). Fiverr keeps a 20% commission on every order.
7. Enter your VAT details (company number), otherwise Fiverr charges VAT on its commission.

**FAQ:**
- *Do I need to write the texts myself?* No. In Standard and Premium I write the copy from our conversation. You review and correct.
- *Can the site be in two languages?* Yes. Dutch, French and English. Included in Premium, available as an extra on other packages.
- *Do you do hosting and the domain name?* I set it up for you (Netlify, Cloudflare or your own hosting). Hosting and domain costs are paid by you; I can run them for you for a monthly fee.

- *What do you need from me to start?* Your business name, what you do, your opening hours and any photos or logo you have. The rest we sort out in a few messages.
- *What counts as "Next level"?* Anything beyond a brochure site: online booking, a product catalogue or small shop, customer accounts, payment or calendar integrations, custom animations. Message me first so I can quote correctly.
- *Can you deliver faster?* Basic can be done in 24 hours as an extra. For Standard and Premium, ask before ordering.

## 5. Getting the first reviews
- Ask 2 or 3 people you know who need a site to order through Fiverr (with a discount via a custom offer). The first reviews carry a lot of weight.
- Reply within the hour: your response time is shown on your profile.
- Always deliver before the deadline, never on it.
- After delivery, ask once, politely, for a review.

## 6. Pitfalls
- **Never** communicate or get paid outside Fiverr (no email, WhatsApp or direct payment) until the order is complete. That gets accounts suspended.
- Don't promise Google rankings ("rank #1"). Nobody can guarantee that, and Fiverr removes such gigs.
- No fake reviews or invented clients in the portfolio. The five example sites are labelled "concept" and stay that way.
